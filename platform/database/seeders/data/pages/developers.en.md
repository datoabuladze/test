Have you made an HTML5, WebGL, Unity WebGL or Phaser game? We would love to hear from you.

## What we publish
- Games you own, or have the right to license to us.
- HTML5 / JavaScript, Phaser, Unity WebGL builds, or an embeddable URL hosted by you.
- Games suitable for a general audience. No gambling mechanics.

## What we need from you
- The game files or an embed URL, and a short description.
- The license or permission you grant: hosting method, whether ads may be shown, attribution text.
- Thumbnail artwork you own.

## What you get
- Credit and a link on the game page.
- Statistics about plays and load failures on request.
- Removal at any time on request.
