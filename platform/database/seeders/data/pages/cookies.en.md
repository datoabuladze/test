This page explains the cookies and similar technologies :brand uses.

## Strictly necessary
| Name | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| Session cookie | Keeps you logged in and protects forms against cross-site request forgery. | Until you close the browser (or 2 hours of inactivity) |
| Remember-me cookie | Only if you tick "Remember me" when logging in. | Up to 5 years or until you log out |
| Room seat cookie | Remembers your seat in a private two-player room so you can reconnect. | Up to 8 hours |

These cookies are required for the website to work and do not need consent.

## Stored in your browser
We keep a few preferences in your browser's local storage: light/dark theme, the sound setting of the player, your list of recently played games and your cookie choice. This data never leaves your device.

## Optional (only with your consent)
If this deployment enables analytics (Google Analytics 4) or advertising (Google AdSense), the related cookies are set **only after you accept** them in the cookie banner. You can change your choice at any time by clearing your browser's storage for this site.

## Games
Games run in an isolated sandbox and cannot set cookies for :brand. Embedded partner games may use their own storage according to the partner's policy.
