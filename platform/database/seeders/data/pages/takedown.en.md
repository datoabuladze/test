We respect the rights of game creators and other rights holders. If you believe content on :brand infringes your copyright or trademark, tell us and we will act quickly.

## How to send a notice
Email **:contact_email** with:
1. your name and contact details, and whether you are the rights holder or authorized to act for them;
2. the work you believe is infringed;
3. the exact :brand page URL(s);
4. a statement that you believe in good faith the use is not authorized;
5. a statement that the information in your notice is accurate.

## What happens next
- We confirm receipt, usually within two business days.
- Clearly infringing content is unpublished while we review it.
- If the game was supplied by a partner, we forward the notice to them.
- If you were the person who supplied the content and believe it was removed by mistake, you can send a counter-notice to the same address.

You can also use the **Report** button on any game page and choose "Copyright or license concern".
