## Do I need an account?
No. You can play every game as a guest. A free account lets you save favorites, appear on leaderboards, earn XP and unlock achievements.

## Do games work on my phone?
Most do. Look for the phone icon on game cards or the **Mobile** filter. Some classic games need a keyboard; their pages show a notice on touch devices.

## A game doesn't load. What can I do?
Reload the page and press Play again. Check that your browser is up to date. If it still fails, press **Report** on the game page and pick "The game does not load" so we can fix it.

## Why are some scores "verified"?
For games that support it, we replay your moves on our server to make sure the score is real. Verified scores go on the main leaderboard; other scores appear on the casual board.

## Are the games really free?
Yes. There are no paid games and no real-money gambling. Some pages may show ads to keep the website running.

## How do I delete my account?
Go to **Account → Settings → Delete account**. Your profile and progress are deleted immediately.

## Can I play with a friend?
Yes. Open **Play with a friend**, create a room and send the link. Several games also have a two-player mode on one device.
