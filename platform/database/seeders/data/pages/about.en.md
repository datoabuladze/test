:brand is a home for **free browser games** that start in a second. No downloads, no installs and no plugins: press Play and the game runs on your computer, tablet or phone.

## What makes :brand different
- **Original games.** Our studio makes its own games, from relaxed puzzles to fast arcade challenges.
- **Licensed catalog.** Every third-party game has documented permission from its creator.
- **Safe by design.** Games run in an isolated sandbox, away from your account and personal data.
- **Fair leaderboards.** Competitive scores are checked on our servers, and casual scores are labelled separately.
- **Four languages.** :brand speaks English, Georgian, Turkish and Russian.
- **No gambling.** Ever.
