Every game on :brand is either an **original game** made for :brand or a third-party game published with **documented permission**. We never publish a game just because it is playable or downloadable somewhere else on the internet.

## How a third-party game gets published
1. **Source recorded:** the game's title, developer, source URL and the exact license or distribution agreement are recorded.
2. **Rights checked:** we confirm the permitted hosting method (self-hosted or embedded), commercial-use and advertising terms, attribution requirements, allowed modifications and thumbnail rights.
3. **Technical check:** the game is launched in our sandboxed player and checked on desktop and mobile.
4. **Approval:** only after a staff member marks the rights as verified can the game be published. Games with missing or unclear rights stay hidden.

## Flash classics
Flash games run through Ruffle, an open-source emulator, so no Flash plugin is needed. We only publish SWF files whose authors or rights holders allow redistribution, and only after testing them for compatibility.
