:brand is operated by :operator. This policy explains what personal data we process when you use the website, why, and what choices you have. We designed :brand to work **without an account** and to collect as little data as possible.

## What we collect

**When you play as a guest**
- A daily-rotating, salted hash of your IP address and browser identifier, used only to count unique players and prevent abuse. The hash changes every day and cannot be reversed to identify you.
- The device type (desktop, tablet or phone), the language you use and the website that referred you, stored with each game launch.
- Your recently played games are kept **only in your own browser** (local storage), not on our servers.

**When you create an account**
- Your email address, nickname and a securely hashed password.
- Your favorites, ratings, scores, achievements, XP and play history.
- Optional: an avatar image, which we re-encode before storing.

We do **not** ask for your real name, date of birth, phone number or address.

## Why we use it
- To provide the games and account features you ask for (contract).
- To keep the service secure, prevent cheating and abuse (legitimate interest).
- To understand which games work well and which are broken, using aggregated statistics (legitimate interest).
- To send account emails such as verification and password resets. Optional newsletters are sent only if you opt in.

## Personalization
Recommendations use only your favorites and recent plays on :brand. You can switch personalization off at any time in **Account → Settings**. We do not build advertising profiles.

## Advertising and analytics
Advertising and third-party analytics are **off by default**. If they are enabled on this deployment, they load only after you agree in the cookie banner, and non-personalized ads are used for visitors who decline. Personalized advertising is never shown to users we know to be children.

## Games from third parties
Some games are embedded from authorized partners. Those games run in an isolated, sandboxed frame and cannot read your :brand account, cookies or storage. A partner may process data under its own privacy policy; we link to the partner on each game page.

## Retention
- Game-launch records: up to 25 months, then deleted or aggregated.
- Search queries: stored without any user identifier, up to 12 months.
- Account data: until you delete your account. Deleting your account removes your profile, favorites, ratings, scores and achievements immediately; anonymous play statistics remain.

## Your rights
Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data, to object to processing, and to complain to a data-protection authority. You can delete your account yourself in **Account → Settings**. For any other request, email **:contact_email**.

## Children
:brand is a general-audience website. If you are under the age required by the law of your country to consent to data processing, ask a parent or guardian before creating an account. We do not knowingly collect more data from children than is needed to provide the service.

We will post any changes on this page and update the date at the top.
